Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective DRAFT-DATE · For the Collared app · Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws

This page covers the information in Collared that relates to your body or your health. It sits beside our privacy policy, which covers everything else. Where the two differ on health data, this page is the more specific one. Collared is for people 18 and over.

What health data we collect, and why

  • Water and caffeine. The drinks you log: what, how much, caffeine, and when. Why: to run a water task, show your progress, and, only if you choose, write the drinks to Apple Health or Health Connect. Private and encrypted where it is stored, readable by you alone, never read by us, and not end-to-end encrypted. If a Dominant asked to be told, a day summary or note goes to the two of you, stored the same way.
  • Medications. Your medicine names, strengths, shapes, dose times, and each dose you take, skip or mark as not sure. Why: to remind you and keep your history. The list and your dose history are private and encrypted where they are stored, behind access rules: you can see them, and the person who leads you sees his own copy, holding only what your agreement lets him see. We do not read them, and they are not end-to-end encrypted. If he edits your list, his edit waits for your phone. A reminder's generic title (such as Morning meds), its time and days, and whether a dose was taken, skipped or marked missed, with the minute, are stored like any task. Collared does not give medical advice and does not read or write Apple Health's medications.
  • Runs, walks and workouts. Each finished workout: route, splits, distance, time, pace, heart rate, steps, climb, the energy you burned, and the app it came from; and, if your agreement allows, your heart rate live while you are out. Why: to show your run to you and to the one person you share it with, and to finish a run task. Stored on our servers in a form we can read.
  • Fitness. Your daily steps with their hourly totals, your records, and the couple ring and season you share with the people you train with. Why: to show your day and your progress to you and to the people you choose to share fitness with. Stored on our servers in a form we can read, behind access rules, and shown only to them.
  • Health reads for checks. With your permission, Collared reads from Apple Health or Health Connect: workouts, steps, wheelchair pushes, whether you use a wheelchair, when you fell asleep, and water from your other apps. Why: to finish a task you chose (for example steps or asleep by) and to keep your water count right. For these checks only the result leaves your phone: that the task was done and the minute, and a short line for you and your partner (for example "Run, 32 min"), stored readable behind access rules, never read by us and not end-to-end encrypted. No Health sample or sleep stage is sent. Weight and cycle can never be used as a check.
  • Health writes. Only if you allow it in Health's own sheet: drinks you log (as water, with caffeine as a separate entry) and runs and walks you track in Collared.
  • Phone permission status. Whether your Health permission is on or off, with the other permissions the app uses. Never what is inside Health.

Where it comes from

From you (what you log and type), from your phone's sensors and Apple Health or Health Connect when you allow them, and from the Apple Watch app where you use it.

Who it is shared with

  • The person you choose. The person who leads you in a dynamic sees the health data your agreement and your own settings allow, such as your run, your water progress, and your medication reminders at the level you agreed to. Nobody else in the app sees it.
  • Companies that run Collared for us. Supabase (database and file storage, hosted in the United States) holds the data described above, stored behind access rules in a form that is readable but that we do not read, as said. Expo, Apple and Google deliver notifications, which carry generic words (never a medicine name). Apple Maps draws maps and names places. These companies process data only to run the service.
  • Never sold, never used for advertising. We do not sell health data, do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, and do not use it for marketing. Crash reports go to Sentry without your account and without health content.
  • Legal orders. We may disclose data when the law requires it, and we will tell you where we are allowed to.

Your choices and rights

  • Consent and withdrawing it. Each Health permission is asked for when a feature needs it. Withdraw it any time in your phone's Health and Settings. Tasks that need it then go to your word instead. Writing to Health is a separate choice you can turn off.
  • Delete. In the app: Delete my medication history removes your dose history and your partner's copy at once; Remove this run removes a run from your history and your partner's; you can remove water entries. Settings › Close my account closes your account straight away and deletes everything, including this data, after 30 days (you can restore it in that time). Backups at our database provider are replaced in the normal course. Anything you wrote into Apple Health stays in Apple Health until you delete it there.
  • Access and a copy. Settings › Take a copy makes your record as a PDF on your phone. For anything else, write to us.
  • Questions and appeals. If we decline a request you can appeal by writing to us, and you may contact your state attorney general.

Contact

Write to hello@collared.app for any request on this page. We will answer within 45 days.
