Your location, your terms

Who sees it,
and who never will.

Being known is the point in a dynamic. It should only ever be known by the person you chose.

Agreed by both of you

Sharing is set once, when your dynamic starts, and both of you agree to it.

Never sold

Your location goes to the person you chose. Never to an advertiser, a data broker or an insurer.

Plainly said

Messages, Glimpses and Story time books are end-to-end encrypted. Location isn't, so it can arrive fast; it's still only for the person you chose.

For comparison: Life360 says it shares location, device and driving data with Arity, an Allstate company, for ads and insurance offers in the US, and that turning this off also turns off its driving features.
