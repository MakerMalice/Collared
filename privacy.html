Privacy policy

Effective 17 September 2026 · Applies to the Collared app and collared.app

Collared is built on a simple idea: what happens in your dynamic is yours. This page says, in plain words, what the app stores, who can see it, and what we will never do with it.

What we store

  • Your account. Email address, a username you choose, an optional display name and avatar, your role, and the date you confirmed you are 18 or older. If you sign in with Apple, Google, or Discord, or link Discord to your account, we receive the identifier, email, and display name those services share; we never see your password for those services.
  • Your dynamics. Who you are connected to, the terms you both accepted (roles, honorifics, structure, rights), and the history of pauses, changes, and endings.
  • Tasks, points, rewards, punishments. Everything assigned, completed, reviewed, redeemed, or issued inside a dynamic, including proof notes and proof photos, plus the points ledger.
  • Your journal. Entries, photos, and the sharing windows you grant. Entry text is stored so it can sync to your devices, but nothing on our side reads it: no search index, no scanning, no automated processing of any kind.
  • Agreements. Files or text you upload as your contract, and who has acknowledged each version. Agreement contents are treated exactly like journal text.
  • Device tokens and preferences. A push-notification token for each device you sign in on, your notification choices, theme, and whether app lock is on.
  • Invite links. Which invite link a new account arrived through, so we can credit the person who shared it. Only the account owner and the person who invited them can see this.
  • Feedback you send us. What you write in "Tell us what you think", plus the build you were on, the screen you came from, and your device and its version. Never anything you have written elsewhere in the app. If you choose to attach a screenshot or a screen recording, whatever is on screen in it comes with it, which is why the app says so before you attach one.

Who can see what

  • Your partner in a dynamic sees what the dynamic's rights allow: tasks and proof they assigned or review, the points ledger, the reward shop, punishments, agreements, and any journal entries you choose to share with them for as long as you choose.
  • Friends see counts: tasks done, points, journal entries written. They see the content of a journal entry only when you set that entry's audience to friends or to them specifically, and you can change that at any time.
  • Everyone else sees nothing. Profiles can be found by exact username or invite code only, never by email, and never by browsing.
  • Blocking is silent and immediate. A blocked person disappears from your app and you from theirs.

What we never do

  • We never read your journal or your agreements. Our servers are built so they cannot, and our team policy is the same.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We run no analytics or tracking software in the app. The only diagnostic data we collect is a crash report when the app crashes, which contains technical details about the failure and never the content of your dynamic.
  • We never look up people by email, and we never show your email to anyone.

Who helps us run Collared

Collared runs on Supabase (database, file storage, and sign-in, hosted in the United States) and sends push notifications through Expo's push service, which hands them to Apple and Google to deliver. These providers process data only to run the service for us. Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in, and Discord sign-in are provided by Apple, Google, and Discord under their own terms.

Retention

  • Proof photos are kept as part of your dynamic's record: full size for 30 days, then a small compressed copy, until the dynamic ends or either of you deletes them. Either person can delete one at any time. Nothing is blurred and nothing is destroyed when a task is approved.
  • Data exports you request are deleted 24 hours after they are generated.
  • Screenshots and recordings attached to feedback are deleted after 90 days. What you wrote in the message is kept, so a decision we made about it still has its reason.
  • A closed account is fully deleted 30 days after you close it. Until then nothing is destroyed and you can restore it yourself.
  • Everything else stays until you delete it or delete your account.

Your controls

  • Export. Settings › Export my data gives you everything you wrote and every connection as one file.
  • Delete. Settings › Delete account closes your account straight away: your dynamics pause, you disappear from the app, and nobody can find you. Nothing is deleted for thirty days. Sign in any time in those thirty days and you can have all of it back exactly as you left it. After thirty days every photo and everything you wrote is deleted for good and cannot be recovered by you or by us. Partners keep only the points history they could already see, with your name removed.
  • Sharing. Every journal share has a window you set, and every share can be revoked at any time.
  • Notifications. Per-type toggles, snooze, and a discreet mode that replaces lock-screen text with "You have an update."
  • App lock. Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode, with nothing shown in the app switcher.

Adults only

Collared is for people 18 and over. We do not knowingly keep accounts for anyone younger, and we remove any we find.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Questions and requests go to hello@collared.app.
