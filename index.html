01 · SMART TASKS

Tasks that
check themselves.

Set it once and their phone does the checking: the run, the steps, the walk home, the photo taken right now. The task closes on its own, so you know it's done even from the other side of the country. Morning rituals run step by step, and a tap on the wrist says when it's time.

  • A check can finish a task. It can never fail one.
  • A photo taken in Collared counts straight away
  • Once, every Tuesday, or every morning at nine
Coming from a habit trackerEverything you tick by hand today, plus the part where the phone does the checking.

02 · POINTS, THE SHOP AND PUNISHMENTS

Earn it, spend it,
answer for it.

Every kept task earns points. Spend them in a shop your Dominant stocks, on things like a lie-in, movie night or a day off. When something slips, the punishment is picked from the list you agreed together, with points taken or a restriction that ends on its own.

  • Featured rewards, and ones that leave on a date
  • Never takes the balance below zero
  • Rules, limits and asks, kept in one place
One ledger, two phonesEvery point, reward and punishment reads the same on both phones, wherever each of you is.

03 · WHERE THEY ARE

Know they
got home.

Live location, agreed once when your dynamic starts. Places tell you when they get there and when they leave. Their day is told back as a little story, and the drive home is a road that ends when the porch light comes on.

  • Expect them by a time, at a place
  • On the road, timers wait while they drive
  • A heads-up when their battery runs low

Shared with the person you chose. Never with an advertiser, a data broker or an insurer.

For the miles in betweenWhen you live apart, you'll know the moment they're home. No texting to check, no asking twice.

04 · HELD

Ask to be Held.
They're there in a breath.

Press and hold in the app, on the Lock Screen or with the Action button. Your person is told at once, and you're already breathing with their voice and their steady beat while they join you from wherever they are.

However far apartHeld reaches them wherever they are, and keeps sharing where you are until you say you're okay.
05 · JOURNALS

Yours,
until you share it.

Pick a book from your shelf and write in your own hand. Tape in a photo, or keep a page closed until a date. Share an entry for a day, a week or for good, and take it back any time. On iPhone Duo it opens like a real book.

All in one appYour journal sits next to your tasks, your messages and your day together. One app holds the lot, so there's nothing else to keep up with.
06 · WORKOUTS

Move together,
even apart.

Start anything from a run to the rower, and lift to the targets your Dominant set. Run in two different cities and they follow along live, and their cheer lands on your wrist mid-stride. A long-distance morning run can still be one you do together.

  • Apart or side by side
  • Steps and goals they set, saved to Health
Together, miles apartYou see their pace as it happens, and one tap sends a cheer to their wrist.
07 · WATER, MEDS AND CYCLE

Looked after,
every day.

The whole page is the water. Each drink you log sinks to its own band, and the day ends up in layers. Meds sit in a weekly pill case, with Taken right on the reminder and the last dose always in view. Cycle tracking keeps the day of the cycle, how you feel and when the next period is likely, and you choose who sees it.

Looking after each otherFrom a different town, one look says they've had their water and taken their meds. A skipped dose never ends a streak.
08 · READ TOGETHER

The same page,
miles apart.

Bring your own books or open a classic. See who's ahead, pass a page with a note, and read the bedtime stories written for two.

  • Your EPUBs and PDFs, shared with the people you choose
  • Story time books are end-to-end encrypted
09 · GLIMPSES

A glimpse of your day,
for one person.

A photo of right now, sent to the one person it's for. When you're apart it's the next best thing to being there. Private ones stay blurred until they're tapped, and every Glimpse is end-to-end encrypted.

Taken liveGlimpses are taken with the camera in Collared, never picked from the camera roll, so what arrives really is this moment.
Private on purpose

What's yours
stays yours.

Your messages, Glimpses and Story time books are end-to-end encrypted. They're locked on your phone before they leave it, and only the people you share them with can open them. We can't read them either. No ads, no tracking, and nothing about you is ever sold.

Encrypted on your phoneApp lock and a discreet iconNo ads, nothing sold
