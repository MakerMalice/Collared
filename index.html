Part one · Structure

What's asked, what's earned, and what's owed.

Smart tasks

Tasks that
check themselves.

Set a task once and their phone confirms it: the run, the steps, getting home, a photo taken right now. It closes on its own, so you know it's done.

  • A check can finish a task. It can never fail one.
  • Morning rituals, one step at a time
  • Once, every Tuesday, or every morning at nine
  • A tap on the wrist when it's time
Coming from a habit trackerEverything you tick by hand today, with the phone doing the checking.
Done when

Luna runs 5 kmLuna's home by 6:00 pmLuna taps the tag by the door8,000 steps by 9:00 pmA photo, taken right nowLuna says it's done

Their phone does the counting. You see it's done.
Points, the shop and punishments

Earn it, spend it,
answer for it.

Every kept task earns points to spend in a shop your Dominant stocks: a slow morning, movie night, a day off. When something slips, the punishment comes from the list you agreed together.

  • Featured rewards, and ones that leave on a date
  • Points taken, or a restriction that ends on its own
  • The balance never drops below zero
  • Rules, limits and asks, kept in one place
One ledger, two phonesEvery point, reward and punishment reads the same for both of you.

Part two · Closeness

Near each other, wherever you each are.

Where they are

Know they
got home.

Live location, agreed once when your dynamic starts. Places tell you when they arrive and when they leave, and the drive home is a road that ends when the porch light comes on.

  • Expect them by a time, at a place
  • Their day, told back as a little story
  • Timers wait while they drive
  • A heads-up when their battery runs low

Shared with the person you chose. Never with an advertiser, a data broker or an insurer.

For the miles in betweenWhen you live apart, you'll know the moment they're home. No texting to check, no asking twice.
Held

Ask to be Held.
They're there in a breath.

Press and hold in the app, on the Lock Screen or with the Action button. Your person is told at once, and you breathe with their voice and their steady beat until they're with you.

Held keeps sharing where you are until you say you're okay.

Glimpses

A glimpse of your day,
for one person.

A photo of right now, sent to the one person it's for. Private ones stay blurred until they're tapped, and every Glimpse is end‑to‑end encrypted.

Taken liveGlimpses are taken with the camera in Collared, never picked from the camera roll, so what arrives really is this moment.
Read Together

The same page,
miles apart.

Bring your own books or open a classic. See who's ahead, pass a page with a note, and read bedtime stories written for two.

  • Your EPUBs and PDFs, shared with the people you choose
  • Story time books are end‑to‑end encrypted

Part three · Care

Body, mind and the little things, looked after.

Workouts

Move together,
even apart.

Start anything from a run to the rower, and lift to the targets your Dominant set. They follow your run live, and their cheer lands on your wrist mid-stride.

  • Side by side, or in two different cities
  • Steps and goals they set, saved to Health
A cheer, mid-runYou see their pace as it happens, and one tap sends a cheer to their wrist.
Water, meds and cycle

Looked after,
every day.

The whole page is the water. Each drink sinks to its own band, and by evening the day sits in layers.

  • MedsA weekly pill case, with Taken right on the reminder
  • CycleThe day, how you feel, and when the next period is likely
  • Yours to shareYou choose who sees your cycle
  • Kind streaksA skipped dose never ends one
Journals

Yours,
until you share it.

Pick a book from your shelf and write in your own hand. Tape in a photo, keep a page closed until a date, and share an entry for a day, a week or for good.

  • Take a shared entry back any time
  • On iPhone Duo it opens like a real book
All in one appYour journal sits next to your tasks, your messages and your day together, so there's nothing else to keep up with.
Sub Studio

Dressed
by you.

They snap their clothes and each piece becomes a sticker in a wardrobe. You dress them for the day, pick their nail colours, and can send the look as a surprise to open the night before.

Private on purpose

What's yours
stays yours.

Your messages, Glimpses and Story time books are end‑to‑end encrypted. They're locked on your phone before they leave it, and only the people you share them with can open them. We can't read them. No ads, no tracking, and nothing about you is ever sold.
